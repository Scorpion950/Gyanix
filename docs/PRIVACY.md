# Privacy Policy & Data Handling — Gyanix

## Core Principles
1. **Calm & Non-Intrusive:** No tracking of online/offline status, last seen, typing indicators, or read receipts.
2. **Honest & Explicit:** AI features are always explicitly labelled with a violet spark icon and badge.
3. **User Control:** Users can set their profile to Public, Friends Only, or Private, and selectively toggle activity, paths, and streak visibility.
4. **AI Privacy Safeguards:** Free-tier AI models can train on API submissions unless protected. Gyanix enforces strict filters before sending any context to AI providers.

---

## What We Send to AI Providers
When generating roadmaps, solving doubts, or recommending content, Gyanix sends only:
- General topics and keywords (e.g., "PostgreSQL indexing", "React concurrency")
- High-level skill levels (e.g., "Intermediate")
- Stated career goals (e.g., "Full-Stack Engineer")

### What We NEVER Send to AI Providers
- User real names, usernames, or emails
- Private messages, study room transcripts, or chat logs
- Direct identifiers of other users, friends, or contacts
- Credentials, tokens, or security hashes

### AI Personalisation Opt-Out
Users can disable AI personalisation in **Settings -> Privacy**. When disabled:
- The AI service operates purely on the prompt text entered into the doubt solver or search box.
- No profile history, completed paths, or past assessment data is attached to the request.

---

## Data Retention & Deletion
- **Deactivation:** Instantly hides user profiles, social relationships, and posts from public search and feeds.
- **Permanent Deletion:** Permanently deletes account record, clears storage assets, and unlinks all generated activity.
- **Data Export:** Accessible from Settings, generating a machine-readable JSON archive of user paths, notes, and activity.
